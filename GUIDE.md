# Kept in 2 minutes

One rule: **whatever you don't spend is saved.**
You tell Kept what you have, what comes in and what goes out. It does the maths.

## Set up once

1. **Wallets & cards → Add account.** One for each place your money sits (card, cash, savings).
   In "Money already in it", type what it holds *right now*.
2. **Income → New income.** One for each salary: name, amount, pay day, and which account it lands in.

That's all. Your total is simply what your accounts hold.

## Every day: the big + button

(On a computer it's **Add**, top right.)

- **Expense**: type the amount, pick a category if you like, tap *Add expense*.
- **Income**: when a salary arrives, open **Income** and tap *Record payment*.
- **Exchange**: changed dollars into manat? Enter what you paid and what you got. Kept works out the rate.
- **Transfer**: moved money between your own accounts. This is not an expense.

## Where to look

- **Overview**: your total money, what you saved this month, which salary it came from, what is in each wallet.
- **Savings & goals**: your total savings. *Set aside* puts money towards a goal. It stays in your total; it just stops counting as free to spend.
- **Analytics**: charts, if you're curious.

## When a number looks wrong

- **Balance doesn't match your bank?** Wallets → the account → **Update balance** → type what it really holds.
  Don't use *Edit* for this: Edit only changes the amount the account *started* with.
- **Typed something wrong?** Tap the entry, then change or delete it. There's an *Undo*.
- **Can't find something?** Look at the date at the top. It usually says *This month*.
- **"Saved" looks negative?** It's this month's income you recorded minus what you spent. Record your salary and it will rise.

## Good to know

- Everything stays on your computer. Now and then: **Settings → Your data → Download backup**.
- **Try demo data** (sidebar, or **More** on the phone) lets you explore with made-up numbers. It never mixes with yours.
- **On your iPhone**, open your online copy in Safari → Share → *Add to Home Screen*. It then opens full screen like an app. Downloads (backup, CSV) open the share sheet: pick *Save to Files*.
- If your copy is **online**, it asks for your password (5 wrong tries pause sign-in for 15 minutes). *Settings → Security → Sign out* locks it again. (See [DEPLOY.md](DEPLOY.md).)
